Table: customers

Description:
Stores customer information.

Columns:
customer_id
first_name
last_name
email
phone_number
date_of_birth
gender
address
city
state
country
postal_code
registration_date
loyalty_points
customer_status
created_at
updated_at

Relationships:
One customer can place many orders.
One customer can write many reviews.
One customer can have many wishlist items.
