Table: products

Description:
Stores product information.

Columns:
product_id
product_name
category_id
supplier_id
description
price
stock_quantity
sku
weight
created_at
updated_at

Relationships:
Each product belongs to one category.
Each product is supplied by one supplier.
One product can appear in many order items.
One product can have many reviews.
One product can have inventory records.
One product can appear in many wishlists.
